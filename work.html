001

ATLAS — Satellite imagery pipeline

Earth Observation · Data Engineering

End-to-end pipeline for Sentinel-2-style imagery: scene ingestion, atmospheric correction, cloud masking, tiling and conversion to cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs, cataloged with STAC. Reproducible and scriptable — a new scene becomes an analysis-ready layer without a human touching it.

  • Role — Design & implementation of the pipeline
  • Stack — Python, NumPy, rasterio, COG/STAC, CLI, GitHub Actions CI
  • Outcome — From raw scene to analysis-ready layer in one command; tested and reproducible

002

MUTATIO — Change detection on satellite imagery

AI · Computer Vision · EO

A Siamese U-Net that reads pairs of satellite scenes over time and flags what actually changed: new constructions, vegetation loss, water extent. Trained on annotated tiles with F1/IoU-optimized metrics and packaged for inference, so analysts can run any area of interest and get change maps with confidence scores, not just pixels.

  • Role — Model design, training and evaluation
  • Stack — PyTorch, Siamese U-Net, NumPy, rasterio, GitHub Actions CI
  • Outcome — Territory changes detected on fresh scene pairs; review effort focused on the anomalies, not the whole image

003

TERRA VISION — Geodata in immersive 3D

XR · Geospatial · Real-time 3D

A WebXR environment that turns terrain and satellite-style data into a walkable landscape: procedural terrain draped in imagery layers, and direct interaction — move through the region, scrub through time between two scene states, measure distances by picking points in space. Geodata stops being a flat window and becomes a place.

  • Role — XR engineering & interaction design
  • Stack — Three.js, WebXR, vanilla JS, GitHub Pages
  • Outcome — Immersive review of territory data in the browser, no install; works on desktop and VR headsets
