The chatbot league table reshuffles

19 Jan 2026 Source: Similarweb (via analysis) AI · XR

A year ago the chatbot market was a one-horse race — ChatGPT at ~87% of global generative-AI traffic. The latest Similarweb tracker puts it under 70%, with Google's Gemini above 18% after its search-integration push, DeepSeek at around 4%, and Claude, Perplexity and Grok splitting meaningful minorities. Six months changed the league table more than the previous two years.

Why fragmentation is the story

For builders, the practical consequence is that "which model?" is again a real question: the default choice no longer wins by default, and distribution (search, Android, iOS, super apps) matters as much as benchmark scores. Gemini's rise is the clearest case — capability got it into the game, placement got it the share.
