Claude Opus 4.6 — and the week AI stocks spooked Wall Street

02 Feb 2026 Source: Bloomberg AI · XR

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.6, pitching a fundamental shift in how AI handles complex workplace tasks — with finance and research use cases front and center. The same week, its product cadence (plus OpenAI's) was blamed in part for a software-stock selloff that wiped hundreds of billions in market value: if AI can replace stand-alone business apps, what are those apps worth?

Model releases as market events

The odd new reality: a model release is now a macro event. Anthropic and OpenAI both ran Super Bowl ads this week — the rivalry has graduated from benchmarks to prime-time brand wars, and Wall Street is treating every release as a signal about which platforms survive the agent era.
