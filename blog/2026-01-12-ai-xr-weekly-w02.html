Meta 'right-sizes' VR: Reality Labs layoffs and the AI pivot

12 Jan 2026 Source: The Verge AI · XR

The week's clearest signal about where XR is going came from the company that bet biggest on it. Meta confirmed layoffs across Reality Labs — roughly 10 percent of the division by most reports, with several VR studios shut down — and framed the move as a pivot from horizon-worlds content to AI-powered wearables.

Reading the pivot

The glass-half-full take: VR is moving from overhyped platform bet to right-sized niche, while Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses carry the division's hopes — with reports of production capacity doubling. The pessimist's version: the division has lost tens of billions of dollars, and "wearables" is where the money goes to hide. Either way, the center of gravity moved from immersive worlds to AI on your face.
