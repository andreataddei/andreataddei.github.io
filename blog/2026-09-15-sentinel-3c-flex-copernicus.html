Sentinel-3C and FLEX ride to orbit together

15 Sep 2026 Source: ESA EO · Copernicus

A Vega-C lifted off from Kourou carrying two Earth-observation spacecraft into the same orbit: Copernicus Sentinel-3C and ESA's FLEX. A shared ride is fitting — the two missions are designed to work as a pair.

What each brings

  • Sentinel-3C: continuity for the ocean record — sea-surface temperature, colour, wave height and currents, plus land-cover products used for yield optimization and food-security early warning. It's the third of its family, launched to keep the series unbroken: the least glamorous and most important job in EO.
  • FLEX: the first mission to map solar-induced chlorophyll fluorescence — the faint glow plants emit while photosynthesizing. Paired with Sentinel-3's measurements, it turns the carbon cycle from something we estimate into something we watch.
  • The pairing is the point. FLEX's fluorescence products are calibrated against Sentinel-3's data stream; one launch, one orbit, one combined science case.

While US Earth-science budgets face cuts, Europe keeps launching the instruments that make the global climate record continuous. Data pipelines care about exactly one thing here: nothing breaks the time series.
