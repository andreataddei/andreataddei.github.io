Parmitano named Artemis III pilot — first European on an Artemis crew

09 Jun 2026 Source: The Aviationist Space · Artemis

NASA named the Artemis III crew: commander Randy Bresnik, pilot Luca Parmitano, and mission specialists Andre Douglas and Frank Rubio. Parmitano — flying for ESA — becomes the first European assigned to an Artemis mission, and as pilot of the Orion spacecraft he will be at the controls for the mission meant to put humans back on the lunar surface, currently targeting 2027.

The European angle

  • A first for Europe in the piloting seat. Not just a crew slot: the pilot role during rendezvous, descent preparation and return burns.
  • ESA's Artemis dividends. Europe built Orion's service module — the power, propulsion and life-support backbone of every Artemis flight. Astronaut assignments are the return on that hardware bet.
  • The Italian thread. Parmitano called Italy his "launchpad" into space — and Italy's aerospace industry, from avionics to pressurized modules, now extends to the crew itself.

Artemis III will stay closer to Earth than II did — its ambition is not distance but a landing. For Europe, the message is different: the era of watching from the sidelines is over.
