Samsung shows its AI glasses hand; MWC turns into an XR showcase

02 Mar 2026 Source: CNBC AI · XR

The story

Samsung revealed the first official details of its AI smart glasses: camera, on-device AI assistant, and a lightweight form factor clearly aimed at the Ray-Ban Meta niche rather than bulky headsets. The move confirms that the next battleground for consumer AI is the face.
