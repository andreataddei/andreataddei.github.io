Kimi K3: China's Moonshot ships the largest open-weight model ever

20 Jul 2026 Source: VentureBeat AI · Open Source

Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model it describes as the largest ever openly released, benchmarking against the top closed systems from US labs. Within days the company paused new subscriptions — demand outran its compute, a form of flattery no marketing budget can buy.

Why it lands harder than the benchmark numbers

  • Open weights at frontier scale. The release puts the "can we run it ourselves?" question back on the table for teams that need control, privacy or fine-tuning — at the cost of serious hardware.
  • Compute as the real moat. Serving a 2.8T model is an infrastructure problem; the subscription pause is a reminder that serving capacity, not model quality, is now a bottleneck.
  • The pattern repeats. DeepSeek in 2025, Zhipu's GLM-5.2 in June, Kimi K3 in July — under US chip export limits, China's ecosystem keeps producing capable open models, each one testing Western assumptions about the gap.

For builders, the practical takeaway: an open 2.8T model you can't afford to self-host is still a pricing signal for every closed model you rent.
