DeepSeek V4 looms — and Nasdaq gets nervous

23 Feb 2026 Source: CNBC AI · XR

Reports that DeepSeek's V4 release is imminent had analysts openly warning about a rough patch for Nasdaq stocks — a year after the original DeepSeek moment wiped a trillion dollars off the market. The new normal: an open-source model release is a market-risk event, and everyone from hedge funds to chipmakers prices it in.

The accusation underneath the anxiety

The same week, Anthropic said it had uncovered campaigns by three Chinese firms to illicitly extract capabilities from Claude via distillation — "industrial-scale intellectual property theft," in its words. Distillation is standard practice inside every lab; the fight is over doing it to someone else's model without permission. How that line gets enforced — technically or legally — is now an open policy question.
