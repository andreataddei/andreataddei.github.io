Android XR lands: Samsung's Galaxy XR takes CES

05 Jan 2026 Source: Google (official blog) AI · XR

The year in immersive tech opened with a platform story: Samsung's Galaxy XR, built with Google and Qualcomm, is the first consumer headset to run Android XR — Google's operating system for headsets and glasses, with Gemini woven through it. Google took over the Las Vegas Sphere to make the point.

Why Android XR matters more than any single headset

The bet is the same one that made Android dominant on phones: don't sell one device, sell the platform everyone else builds on. With Qualcomm supplying silicon and Samsung first out the gate, Google is positioning AI — not gaming, not the metaverse — as the reason to wear a headset.
