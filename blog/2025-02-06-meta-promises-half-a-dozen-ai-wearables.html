Meta Promises Half a Dozen AI Wearables

06 Feb 2025 Source: Gizmodo XR · AI

The most telling XR roadmap of early 2025 was not a headset. Meta was reported to be preparing half a dozen AI wearables for the year — a portfolio strategy aimed straight at the face.

The lineup logic is classic consumer electronics: cheap audio-only glasses for the mass market, camera models as the volume seller, and premium display versions for early adopters. Somewhere in between sit sports-focused variants chasing a different demographic entirely.

Contrast that with VR. New Quest headsets were in the works, but the investment gravity had shifted. Glasses scale like consumer products; headsets scale like gaming consoles. Meta's spending followed the distinction.

For the industry, the flood matters as much as the flagships. Each price tier doubles the addressable audience and generates data about what people actually do with AI on their face — the single most valuable dataset nobody had before.

The strategic read: Meta is not betting on one device to win the next platform, but on being present at every tier while the use case crystallizes. When the killer app for wearable AI appears, they intend to already own the hardware it runs on.

The portfolio also hedges the category's biggest unknown. Nobody knows whether the winning form factor is display, camera-only or audio — so Meta's answer is to ship all of them and let the market, not the lab, pick the winners. Wearables also compound in a way headsets never did: a customer who buys one glasses tier is a warm lead for the next, and every line shares the same assistant, lens partners and retail channels.
