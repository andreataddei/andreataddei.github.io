Foundation models meet satellite imagery

14 Oct 2026 Source: NASA Earth Observatory AI · EO

The wave of foundation models trained on overhead imagery keeps growing, and the pitch is always the same: pretrain on huge archives, fine-tune on your small labeled set, get results that used to require thousands of labels.

The interesting part

For tasks like segmentation and change detection over medium-resolution imagery, the approach genuinely changes the economics: a few hundred labels can now beat a from-scratch model trained on tens of thousands, provided the domains are close enough.

The usual caveats

  • Sensor and resolution shifts still hurt: a model pretrained on one satellite family does not automatically transfer to another.
  • Cloud masking and atmospheric conditions remain the unglamorous 80% of the work in practice.
  • Ground truth is expensive — which is exactly why the technique matters.

The takeaway for pipeline builders: keep your fine-tuning data curated and versioned, because it is now the moat.
